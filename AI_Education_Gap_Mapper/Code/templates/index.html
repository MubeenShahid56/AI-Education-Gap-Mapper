Project Overview & Methodology

Problem Statement

The rapid growth of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has increased the demand for professionals with industry-relevant skills. However, many university AI programs focus primarily on theoretical knowledge and may not cover the practical skills, tools, and technologies required by employers.

Proposed Solution

The AI Education Gap Mapper automatically analyzes university course content using Machine Learning models. It maps course descriptions to specific AI sub-domains and maps them against current industry requirements scraped from live job postings to identify educational gaps.

Workflow

  1. Job Data Filtering: Standardize and extract 14,000+ technology/AI related job requirements from over 1.2 million postings.
  2. Model Training: Build classification models (Naive Bayes, Decision Tree, Random Forest) using TF-IDF features.
  3. Syllabus Processing: Extract academic topics and skills using regex-based natural language patterns.
  4. Gap Analysis: Compare academic coverage with the top-ranking skills in the predicted industry domain.

Limitations & Future Work

  • Data Quality Dependency: The accuracy of results depends on the quality, structure, and detail level of course syllabi and descriptions.
  • Temporal Shifts: Industry requirements change frequently. The skill demand database must be re-run periodically to capture emerging technologies (e.g., LLMs, Generative AI).
  • Synonym & Terminology Match: Different educational institutions use varying titles for identical concepts (e.g., "Deep Neural Architectures" vs "Deep Learning").
  • Soft Skills Gap: The mapper is optimized for technical skills (tools, programming languages, algorithms) and may not fully evaluate soft skills like communication or leadership.
